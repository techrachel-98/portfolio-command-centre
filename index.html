About

Turning AI capability into tools that get used

I'm transitioning into AI Automation and AI Engineering roles, building full-stack projects that turn AI capability into practical, working tools.

My focus is agentic systems and automation — designing, building, and shipping end-to-end, from architecture to deployment. I care about tools that actually get used, not demos that stay demos.

That splits into two things I build. One is automation — workflows and agents that connect AI to real systems and trigger real actions, most of it wired through n8n. The other is software with AI built in — full-stack applications where an LLM API is a core part of the product, not a bolt-on feature.

  • Architecture to deploymentI own the whole path — design, build, ship — not just the prototype.
  • Built to be usedReal workflows, real triggers, real users — not screenshots and slide decks.
  • Agentic by defaultLLM orchestration, memory, and automation wired into systems that act.
